# Classifications of knowledge by tradition, plus three older Western maps

How a tradition sorted its own books is a map of what it thought knowledge was. Scan the
scheme that the authors and readers of your corpus used, not only the modern one. Each block
starts with the page it was checked against; items that could not be reached are marked
(unverified). Codes and labels are given in the original script with an English gloss.

## China

### 四庫全書總目 (Siku quanshu zongmu, 1782): 4 部, 44 類, 66 屬

Source: https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/四庫全書總目提要 (the text, by 卷) and
https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/四庫全書 (table of 類 and 屬) (accessed 2026-08-25)

The 總目 is the summit of the four-part scheme: 3,503 works copied, 6,793 more listed by
title only (存目), about 3,100 banned. 屬 are given in brackets.

- 經部 jing, classics (10 類): 易 Changes · 書 Documents · 詩 Odes · 禮 Rites [周禮 · 儀禮 ·
  禮記 · 三禮總義 general works on the three ritual classics · 通禮 comprehensive ritual ·
  雜禮 miscellaneous ritual] · 春秋 Spring and Autumn · 孝經 Filial Piety · 五經總義 general
  works on the Five Classics · 四書 Four Books · 樂 music · 小學 philology [訓詁 glosses ·
  字書 character dictionaries · 韻書 rime books].
- 史部 shi, histories (15 類): 正史 standard histories · 編年 annals · 紀事本末 topical
  narratives · 別史 alternative histories · 雜史 miscellaneous histories · 詔令奏議 edicts and
  memorials [詔令 · 奏議] · 傳記 biography [聖賢 sages · 名人 notables · 總錄 collective ·
  雜錄 miscellaneous] · 史鈔 historical excerpts · 載記 records of non-legitimate and foreign
  regimes · 時令 seasonal calendars · 地理 geography [宮殿 palaces · 總志 empire-wide
  gazetteers · 都會郡縣 local gazetteers · 河渠 waterways · 邊防 frontier defence · 山川
  mountains and rivers · 古蹟 ancient sites · 雜記 miscellanea · 遊記 travel · 外記 foreign
  lands] · 職官 offices [官制 organisation · 官箴 admonitions to officials] · 政書
  institutional compendia [通制 general · 典禮 rites · 邦計 finance · 軍政 military · 法令 law ·
  考工 crafts] · 目錄 catalogues [經籍 books · 金石 epigraphy] · 史評 historical criticism.
- 子部 zi, masters (14 類): 儒家 Confucians · 兵家 military · 法家 legalists · 農家
  agriculture · 醫家 medicine · 天文算法 astronomy and mathematics [推步 calendrical
  computation · 算書 arithmetic] · 術數 divinatory arts [數學 numerology · 占候 omens · 相宅相墓
  siting of houses and tombs · 占卜 divination · 命書相書 fate and physiognomy · 陰陽五行 yin-yang
  and five phases] · 藝術 arts [書畫 calligraphy and painting · 琴譜 qin scores · 篆刻 seal
  carving · 雜技 miscellaneous skills] · 譜錄 registers of things [器物 objects · 食譜 food ·
  草木鳥獸蟲魚 plants and animals] · 雜家 eclectics [雜學 · 雜考 · 雜說 · 雜品 · 雜纂 · 雜編] ·
  類書 encyclopaedias · 小說家 minor narratives [雜事 anecdotes · 異聞 marvels · 瑣語 trivia] ·
  釋家 Buddhism · 道家 Daoism.
- 集部 ji, belles-lettres (5 類): 楚辭 Songs of Chu · 別集 individual collections
  (sub-arranged by period, 漢至五代 … 國朝) · 總集 anthologies · 詩文評 literary criticism ·
  詞曲 lyrics and arias [詞集 · 詞選 · 詞話 · 詞譜詞韻].

The compilers count 66 屬; the table transcribed here shows 63 (the 總目 also has 別錄 under
傳記 and 南北曲 under 詞曲 for works listed by title only). The scheme is a Confucian state's
order of things: the classics as root, histories and masters as trunk, literature as branches,
with Buddhism and Daoism filed among the masters and 詞曲 last.

### Before the four 部: 七略 and how 經史子集 arose

Source: https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/七略 and https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/四部分類法
(accessed 2026-08-25)

七略 (Liu Xiang 劉向 and Liu Xin 劉歆, completed 5 BCE; 603 authors, 13,219 卷) has six
divisions with 38 kinds, plus the 輯略 (general summary) that makes seven: 六藝略 six arts
(易 · 書 · 詩 · 禮 · 樂 · 春秋 · 論語 · 孝經 · 小學) · 諸子略 masters (儒 · 道 · 陰陽 · 法 · 名 · 墨
· 縱橫 · 雜 · 農 · 小說) · 詩賦略 poetry (屈原賦 · 陸賈賦 · 孫卿賦 · 雜賦 · 歌詩) · 兵書略 military
(權謀 · 形勢 · 陰陽 · 技巧) · 術數略 numbers and omens (天文 · 曆譜 · 五行 · 蓍龜 · 雜占 · 形法) ·
方技略 technical arts (醫經 · 經方 · 房中 · 神仙). It survives abridged as 漢書·藝文志; 王儉's 七志
and 阮孝緒's 七錄 kept the six/seven-fold form. History has no division of its own here: it
sits inside the six arts under 春秋.

The four-part scheme arose from that imbalance. By the Wei–Jin period private histories,
local gazetteers, and clan genealogies had multiplied while military writings shrank, so the
histories inside 六藝略 outnumbered the whole 兵書略. 荀勖 (Western Jin, 中經新簿) recast the
seven into four lettered parts: 甲 classics, 乙 masters (absorbing 諸子, 兵書, 術數, 方技),
丙 histories, 丁 poetry. 李充 (Eastern Jin, 晉元帝四部書目) moved histories to 乙 and masters
to 丙, fixing the order 經史子集 (so 錢大昕 in his preface to the 元史·藝文志). The early-Tang 隋書·經籍志 (李延壽, 敬播 et al.) was the first surviving
catalogue to use the names 經史子集: four 部 and 40 sub-classes, with Buddhist and Daoist
canons appended outside them. Every later dynastic catalogue kept the four names.

## Islamic world

### al-Fārābī, Iḥṣāʾ al-ʿulūm (إحصاء العلوم, Enumeration of the Sciences, 10th c.)

Source: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/al-farabi/ and Okamoto, Al-Fārābī's Theory of
Science and his Iḥṣāʾ al-ʿulūm, https://repository.digital.georgetown.edu/downloads/d0f8d0f8-5d04-421c-8c6c-505a244e98f0
(accessed 2026-08-25)

Five chapters enumerate eight "well-known sciences" (ʿulūm mashhūra): (1) the science of
language (ʿilm al-lisān) and its parts; (2) logic (ʿilm al-manṭiq), in eight parts; (3) the
mathematical sciences (ʿulūm al-taʿālīm): arithmetic, geometry, optics, astronomy, music, the
science of weights, and mechanics (ʿilm al-ḥiyal); (4) natural science (ʿilm ṭabīʿī) and
divine science or metaphysics (ʿilm ilāhī); (5) political science (ʿilm madanī),
jurisprudence (fiqh), and dialectical theology (kalām). Latin versions by Gundissalinus and
Gerard of Cremona carried the order into medieval Europe. It is an Aristotelian-Alexandrian
ladder that admits the religious disciplines only as parts of political science.

### Ibn Khaldūn, Muqaddimah (المقدمة, 1377), chapter VI on the sciences

Source: https://www.muslimphilosophy.com/ik/Muqaddimah/Chapter6/Toc_Ch_6.htm (section list
of the Rosenthal translation) (accessed 2026-08-25)

Two kinds. The traditional or transmitted sciences (naqlī), whose authority is revelation:
the Qurʾānic sciences of interpretation (tafsīr) and readings (qirāʾāt) · Prophetic traditions
(ḥadīth) · jurisprudence (fiqh) with its subdivision inheritance law (farāʾiḍ) · principles of
jurisprudence (uṣūl al-fiqh) with dialectics and controversial questions · speculative theology
(kalām), with the dogmatic schools born of ambiguity in Qurʾān and Sunna · Sufism (taṣawwuf) ·
dream interpretation. The intellectual or philosophical sciences (ʿaqlī), natural to man as a
thinking being: the sciences of numbers (calculation, algebra, business arithmetic,
inheritance shares) · the geometrical sciences (spherical figures, conics and mechanics,
surveying, optics) · astronomy (with astronomical tables; astrology as a subdivision) · logic ·
physics (with medicine and agriculture as subdivisions) · metaphysics · sorcery and talismans
· the secrets of letters · alchemy. He then refutes philosophy, astrology, and alchemy, and
sets the "basic philosophical sciences" at seven: logic; arithmetic, geometry, astronomy,
music; physics; metaphysics. The sciences of the Arabic language (grammar, lexicography,
syntax and style with literary criticism, literature) are treated as instruments of the
others. It is the order of a 14th-century Maghribī jurist: revealed knowledge first, reason
ranked by its distance from it.

### Index Islamicus (Brill; editorial offices in London and Madrid)

Source: https://bibliographies.brillonline.com/browse/index-islamicus (accessed 2026-08-25)

The international classified bibliography of European-language work on Islam and the Muslim
world since 1906, over 750,000 records and 5,000 journals. Brill describes "the well-known
Index Islamicus classification scheme" only by example — Education, Philosophy, Shīʿism,
Sudan, Palestine, Israel, "as well as their subcategories" — so the scheme interleaves
subject and country headings. Its actual top-level list is not on any reachable page
(unverified); the nearest published summary is ProQuest's coverage list
(https://about.proquest.com/en/products-services/islamicus-set-c/): Accounting, Archaeology,
Arts, Bibliography and Scholarship, Economics, Education, Geography, History, Islam, Law,
Literature, Natural and Applied Sciences, Philosophy, Politics, Religion, Social Sciences,
Theology, Travel, Zoology, Middle East. It maps a Western academic field, Islamic studies,
rather than a Muslim ordering of knowledge.

## India

### vidyāsthāna (विद्यास्थान): the 14 and 18 seats of knowledge

Source: https://vedicheritage.gov.in/introduction/ (IGNCA), Madhusūdana Sarasvatī's
Prasthānabheda, https://sanskritdocuments.org/sites/snsastri/PrasthAnabheda-Engl.pdf, and
Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.6, https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-vishnu-purana/d/doc57603.html
(accessed 2026-08-25)

Fourteen (Yājñavalkya Smṛti 1.3): the four Vedas (Ṛg, Yajur, Sāma, Atharva) · the six
Vedāṅgas (Śikṣā phonetics · Kalpa ritual · Vyākaraṇa grammar · Nirukta etymology · Chandas
metre · Jyotiṣa astronomy and astrology) · the four Upāṅgas: Purāṇa (cosmogony, genealogy,
dynastic history) · Nyāya (logic; Vaiśeṣika counted within it) · Mīmāṃsā (exegesis; Vedānta
counted within it) · Dharmaśāstra (law and conduct; the Itihāsas Mahābhārata and Rāmāyaṇa,
and Sāṃkhya, Yoga, Pāśupata, and Vaiṣṇava doctrine are placed under it). Eighteen = the
fourteen plus the four Upavedas, one per Veda: Āyurveda (medicine; Kāmaśāstra included) ·
Dhanurveda (archery and warfare) · Gāndharvaveda (music, dance, drama) · Arthaśāstra (polity;
also law, horse lore, architecture, cooking, the 64 kalās). The fourteen are seats of dharma
as well as of knowledge; the last four are seats of knowledge only. The six darśanas
(āstika systems accepting Vedic authority; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_philosophy):
Sāṃkhya, Yoga, Nyāya, Vaiśeṣika, Mīmāṃsā, Vedānta. Everything is graded by its relation to the
Veda, with medicine, war, music, and statecraft admitted as knowledge but not as dharma.

## Cross-cultural

### HRAF Outline of World Cultures (OWC)

Source: https://ehrafworldcultures.yale.edu/help/GetStarted/owc_c.html and
https://hraf.yale.edu/ehraf-world-cultures/ (accessed 2026-08-25); region letters from HRAF
member-library guides, e.g. https://library.bu.edu/c.php?g=311166&p=2080403

Devised by G. P. Murdock in the 1950s, now organised by region and subregion and covering
"over 2500 cultures". An identifier is region letter + subregion letter + number (NM09 =
Iroquois): A Asia · E Europe · F Africa · M Middle East (including Muslim societies in Africa)
· N North America · O Oceania · R Russia and the former Soviet Union · S South America. The
eHRAF World Cultures database holds 361 cultures, 6,676 documents, 769,002 pages (May 2022;
https://hraf.yale.edu/faq-category/2-ehrafdbs/), "over 360" today, adding 10–20 cultures a
year; eHRAF Archaeology holds 107 traditions. It is a mid-century American culture-area map
in which "Middle East" is defined by religion as much as by geography.

### Brian Deer Classification (First Nations, Canada)

Source: https://guides.library.ubc.ca/Indiglibrarianship/briandeer and
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Deer_Classification_System (accessed 2026-08-25)

Devised by A. Brian Deer (Kahnawá:ke Mohawk) at the National Indian Brotherhood library,
1974–76, and for the Union of BC Indian Chiefs; revised as BDC-BC (McCall and Joseph, 1978–80;
UBCIC 2014) and used, with First Nations House of Learning subject headings, by X̱wi7x̱wa

— rather than around disciplines.

### Handbook of Latin American Studies (HLAS, Library of Congress)

Source: https://guides.loc.gov/handbook-of-latin-american-studies (accessed 2026-08-25)

Bacon (The Advancement of Learning, 1605; De augmentis scientiarum, 1623) divided the
understanding into memory, imagination, and reason, and learning accordingly into history
(natural, civil, ecclesiastical, literary), poesy, and philosophy (divine, natural, human;
natural philosophy split into speculative physics and metaphysics and operative mechanics and
natural magic; human philosophy into body, mind, and civil knowledge). Diderot and d'Alembert
took this over "d'après le chancelier Bacon" as the Système figuré des connaissances
humaines: Mémoire → Histoire (sacrée, civile, naturelle) · Raison → Philosophie (science de
Dieu, science de l'homme, science de la nature) · Imagination → Poésie (narrative,
dramatique, parabolique, with music, painting, sculpture, engraving, and architecture as
poetry in other media). Theology sits under Philosophie, a few nodes from divination and
black magic: the tree makes reason the judge of revelation.
