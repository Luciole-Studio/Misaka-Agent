# Maps of disciplines

Official classifications of research fields, each transcribed from its publisher's current edition and checked on 2026-08-25.
They disagree about where a field sits, what it is called and what counts as one field; that disagreement is the point, so scan several.
Codes precede labels; original-language labels sit beside the English; `(unverified)` marks anything not confirmed against the source.

Source: https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/classifications/australian-and-new-zealand-standard-research-classification-anzsrc/latest-release (accessed 2026-08-25; data cube `anzsrc2020_for.xlsx`, release dated 24 Oct 2025)
